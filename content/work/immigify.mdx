## Context

Immigify is an AI-powered immigration platform. I joined as its first product designer and owned everything a user could touch: the marketing site, the AI agent interfaces, the consultant CRM, and the multi-tenant SaaS dashboards on web and mobile.

## Problem

Consultants handle high-volume immigration casework built on complex compliance workflows. The product had to turn those workflows into task-focused interfaces.

## Key decisions

**Design the system before the screens.** As the only designer, I built the design system early: 100+ tokens, reusable components and accessibility standards. Every new screen got easier, not harder.

**Design the AI agent with the people building it.** I designed the conversational agent interfaces together with the engineering and AI teams, aiming to reduce onboarding friction and improve task completion.
