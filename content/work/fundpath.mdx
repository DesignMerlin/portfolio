<Section id="context" title="Context">

FundPath is a crowdfunding platform that bridges the gap between dreamers and supporters: a place where a cause can be met with collective effort, charity and goodwill, one campaign at a time.

</Section>

<Section id="problem" title="Problem">

Many potential fundraisers and donors struggle to find a platform that feels personalised, intuitive and reliable. They face complex interfaces, a lack of transparency and limited mobile optimisation, which makes supporting or starting a cause cumbersome and discouraging.

</Section>

<Section id="research" title="User research">

I ran an online survey with Google Forms, for people who had actually used a crowdfunding platform before. I wanted to understand donation habits, transparency issues, popular causes and how safe people feel paying.

<Section id="stories" title="User stories">

<Callout title="Donor">

As a donor wanting to make a difference, I want to easily find and support meaningful causes on a trustworthy platform, so that I can contribute to projects that align with my values and see the impact of my donations in real time.

</Callout>

<Callout title="Fundraiser">

As a fundraiser with a specific need, I want to create and manage a fundraising campaign with minimal effort, so that I can quickly raise the funds I need and keep my supporters informed about the progress.

</Callout>

</Section>

<Section id="outcome" title="Outcome">

The design puts campaign creators and donors first, with trust built through real-time updates and transparency reports.

</Section>

<Section id="reflection" title="What I took from it">

**Flexibility made it useful to more people.** Supporting everything from medical emergencies to personal and community projects, with flexible funding so partial contributions still help, let the same product meet very different needs.

**Sharing turned out to be powerful.** Social media integration became a strong way to raise a campaign's visibility, because people can share it in a tap and bring others in.

</Section>
